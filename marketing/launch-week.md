# Launch Week — day-by-day

Assumes the product is functional, payments live, 5 locales shipped,
waitlist has 200+ signups. This is the public push.

Boundaries: Claude drafts everything below. A human (you or a hire) does
the actual posting, replying, and account operation. Marked [HUMAN].

## Pre-launch (Sunday before)

- [ ] Schedule waitlist email blast for Monday 9am local-time waves
- [ ] Pre-record 21 short-form videos (3/day × 7 days)
- [ ] Draft Reddit posts for 6 communities, save in /marketing/reddit/
- [ ] Confirm Sentry, PostHog, Stripe webhooks all firing
- [ ] Dry-run: load-test analyze endpoint at 50 concurrent uploads

## Monday — Waitlist activation

- 9am UTC: [HUMAN] Send waitlist email "It's ready" — segmented by locale
- 12pm UTC: Post launch tweet thread (draft in /marketing/social/x.md)
- 3pm UTC: Post on Hacker News "Show HN" with privacy-first angle
- 6pm UTC: Post first TikTok of the week
- All day: [HUMAN] reply to every comment within 30 minutes

Target: 50 paid conversions from waitlist (25% of 200).

## Tuesday — Product Hunt

- 12:01am PT: [HUMAN] Launch on Product Hunt
- Hunter coordinated 2 weeks prior (NOT an executive last-minute ask)
- First 10 supporters notified to comment in first 30 minutes
- All day: [HUMAN] respond to every PH comment with a real answer
- Side-channel: Indie Hackers post + BetaList submission

Target: Top 5 of the day. 100+ upvotes minimum.

## Wednesday — Reddit

- 9am UTC: r/personalfinance — value post (not promo): "I analyzed 100
  apartment leases. Here are the 7 clauses landlords hope you don't read."
  Mention PaperLens once, in a comment, when relevant.
- 11am UTC: r/legaladvice — answer-first format
- 2pm UTC: r/de — German market post
- 5pm UTC: r/brasil — PT-BR market post
- 8pm UTC: r/india

Rule: every post is 80% value, 20% mention. Mods ban promo-first.

## Thursday — Influencer wave

- [HUMAN] Send 50 personalized DMs to micro-influencers (10K-100K)
  in finance, legal, immigrant, expat, tenant-rights niches
- Offer: 30% rev-share for 6 months, custom landing page per influencer
- Template in /marketing/influencer-outreach.md
- 5 confirmations is a great day. 2 is normal.

## Friday — Press

- Send to: TechCrunch, The Verge, Lifehacker, Wirecutter, local-language
  outlets (Heise.de, NextINpact, Tecmundo, Gigazine)
- Angle: "AI helps non-English speakers read foreign contracts" — human
  interest hook beats "another AI app" pitch
- Press kit linked, screenshots, founder quote, 1 customer testimonial

## Weekend — Watch and learn

- [HUMAN] No new posts. Read every signup's PostHog session replay.
- Identify the top 3 drop-off points. Fix Monday morning.
- Tally: paid conversions, retention day-1, top traffic source.

## Decision after launch week

- > 100 paid: scale the channel that delivered most. Hire VA for support.
- 50-100 paid: keep grinding the same playbook for 4 more weeks.
- < 50 paid: stop. Review activation funnel. Probably product, not marketing.
