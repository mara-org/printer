## Honest scope of what's here vs. what needs a human

Claude wrote everything in this folder. Executing it requires:

- A human to post on social platforms (no API can substitute presence)
- A human to reply to comments and DMs in real time during launch
- A human to authorize ad spend and Stripe configuration
- A human to coordinate with influencers and press

If you want to operate alone, expect 2-3 hours/day for posting + replies
during weeks 1-12. Hiring a VA at $5-8/hr in the Philippines or LATAM
is the standard solo-founder move once MRR > $2K.

What Claude can keep doing on demand:
- Iterate copy based on PostHog data
- Generate new scripts weekly based on what worked
- A/B test landing-page variants
- Translate to additional locales
- Write press pitches and follow-ups
- Draft replies to incoming press / influencer DMs
